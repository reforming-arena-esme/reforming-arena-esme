04 The finale

Close the loop.
Zero flare.

A major energy company is developing an integrated asset — drilling, refining and petrochemicals — and mandates a full transition to a circular economy. No single engineer can solve it alone.

  • Refining designs the flare gas recovery system and routes captured gas downstream.
  • Mud & cementing specifies eco-friendly additives and secures well integrity.
  • Petrochemicals turns the recovered gas into the polymers and additives the well needs.
  • Metallurgy selects corrosion-resistant alloys and protects the whole system.
